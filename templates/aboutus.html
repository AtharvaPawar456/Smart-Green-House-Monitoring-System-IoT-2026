About GreenSense

GreenSense is a Smart Green House Monitoring IoT platform designed for modern agriculture and greenhouse environments. The system collects real-time environmental sensor data including temperature, humidity, soil moisture and rain detection. GreenSense helps monitor agricultural conditions using 'Arduino Nano 33 IoT' based IoT devices with cloud-connected dashboards.
